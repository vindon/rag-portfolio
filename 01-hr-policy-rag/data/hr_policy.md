# Acme Corp — Employee HR Policy Manual
**Version 4.2 | Effective: January 2025 | People & Culture Division**

---

## 1. Annual Leave Policy

### 1.1 Entitlement
All full-time employees are entitled to **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rated basis calculated against a standard 40-hour week.

### 1.2 Leave Accrual
Leave accrues at 1.67 days per completed month of service. New employees joining before the 15th of a month accrue leave for that month. Employees joining on or after the 15th begin accrual from the following month.

### 1.3 Leave Carry-Over
A maximum of **5 days** may be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carry-over must be used by 31 March of the following year. Unused carry-over after this date is forfeited. Exceptions require written approval from the Head of People & Culture.

### 1.4 Requesting Leave
All leave requests must be submitted via the HR portal (hr.acmecorp.internal) at least **10 working days** in advance. Emergency leave requests (fewer than 3 days' notice) require direct manager approval and HR notification within 24 hours.

### 1.5 Blackout Periods
Leave requests will not be approved during Q4 (October–December) product release windows, unless for medical or emergency reasons documented with appropriate evidence.

## 2. Remote Work Policy

### 2.1 Eligibility
Employees who have completed their **90-day probationary period** are eligible to work remotely up to **2 days per week**. This applies to roles where deliverables can be completed independently of physical presence.

### 2.2 Core Hours
All employees, whether remote or in-office, must be available and responsive during **core hours: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM in their local time zone**. Meetings should be scheduled within these hours where possible.

### 2.3 Equipment and Security
The company provides one laptop per employee. Employees working remotely must:
- Use the company VPN at all times when accessing internal systems
- Ensure their home workspace is private and secure
- Not use public Wi-Fi without the VPN active
- Report any security incidents within 2 hours of discovery

### 2.4 Manager Approval
Remote work schedules must be agreed with the direct manager at the start of each month. Ad hoc changes require 24 hours' notice. Managers may revoke remote work privileges for a team member if performance, collaboration, or attendance targets are not being met.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement Policy

### 3.1 Eligible Expenses
The company reimburses reasonable business expenses including:
- **Travel**: flights (economy class), trains, taxis, and ride-sharing for business purposes
- **Accommodation**: up to **$200/night** for domestic travel, **$300/night** for international
- **Meals**: up to **$60/day** when travelling overnight
- **Client entertainment**: up to **$150/person** with manager pre-approval

### 3.2 Submission Deadline
All expense claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be reimbursed unless extenuating circumstances are documented and approved by the CFO.

### 3.3 Required Documentation
- Original receipts for all expenses over $25
- Business purpose stated for each expense
- Client name for entertainment expenses
- Manager signature on the expense form

### 3.4 Monthly Travel Budget
Individual travel expenses must not exceed **$2,000 per month** without prior written approval from the department head. Expenses over $500 require manager pre-approval before being incurred.

### 3.5 Non-Reimbursable Items
The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:
- Alcohol (except client entertainment, pre-approved)
- Personal grooming or clothing
- Gym or fitness memberships
- Fines, penalties, or traffic violations
- Upgrades to business or first class without VP approval

## 4. Parental Leave Policy

### 4.1 Primary Caregiver Leave
Employees who are the primary caregiver for a newborn, newly adopted, or fostered child are entitled to **16 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of their base salary. An additional 4 weeks of unpaid leave may be taken immediately following the paid period.

### 4.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave
Secondary caregivers are entitled to **4 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of their base salary, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or placement of the child.

### 4.3 Eligibility
Employees must have completed **6 months of continuous service** before the expected birth or placement date to qualify for paid parental leave. Employees who do not meet this threshold may take the statutory minimum under applicable law, unpaid beyond the statutory requirement.

### 4.4 Notice Requirements
Employees should notify HR and their manager **at least 8 weeks** before the anticipated commencement of parental leave. A return-to-work plan must be agreed upon before leave begins.

## 5. Code of Conduct

### 5.1 Respect and Inclusion
Acme Corp is committed to a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and bullying. All employees must treat colleagues, clients, and vendors with dignity and respect regardless of race, gender, age, sexual orientation, disability, religion, or any other protected characteristic.

### 5.2 Zero-Tolerance Policy
The following will result in immediate termination without notice:
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Physical violence or threats
- Theft or fraud against the company or colleagues
- Deliberate data breach or misuse of confidential information

### 5.3 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose proprietary business information, client data, pricing, or product roadmaps to external parties without written authorisation. This obligation continues for **2 years** after employment ends.

### 5.4 Conflicts of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal financial interest in a supplier, competitor, or client company to the Ethics Committee within 10 working days of that interest arising. Undisclosed conflicts of interest are grounds for disciplinary action.

## 6. Performance Management

### 6.1 Review Cycle
Performance reviews are conducted **twice yearly**: Mid-year in June and Annual in December. New employees receive a 90-day check-in review during their probationary period.

### 6.2 Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
Employees whose performance is rated "Below Expectations" in two consecutive reviews will be placed on a formal PIP lasting **60 days**. A PIP outlines specific, measurable targets. Failure to meet PIP targets may result in termination with notice.

### 6.3 Recognition
The company operates a quarterly recognition programme. Any employee may nominate a peer for an "Acme Ace" award. Winners receive a $500 gift card and public recognition at the all-hands meeting.

---

## 7. Disciplinary Policy

### 7.1 Verbal Warning
First instance of minor misconduct results in a documented verbal warning, retained in the employee file for 6 months.

### 7.2 Written Warning
Repeat minor misconduct or a first instance of moderate misconduct results in a written warning, retained for 12 months.

### 7.3 Final Written Warning
Serious misconduct or failure to improve after a written warning results in a final written warning. Further misconduct at this stage results in termination.

### 7.4 Right of Appeal
Employees have the right to appeal any disciplinary outcome within **10 working days** by submitting a written appeal to the HR Director. Appeals are heard by a panel that does not include the original decision-maker.
